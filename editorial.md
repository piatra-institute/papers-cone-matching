# Editorial record

Status: full read completed on the final manuscript (2026-10-21); review bound by hash in editorial-review.json.

## Contribution and reader

For specialists in the relative Langlands program, and for readers of Chen and Venkatesh's singular examples: which root data and which global forms make the support comparison at the heart of their Theorem 7.1 work. Confidence: the results are proved in the text and every finite claim is checked in exact arithmetic; novelty is unestablished beyond a targeted search (no erratum to Proposition 5.1 and no classification of the convex reduction was found).

## Material worth preserving

- Lemma 2: equality of cones splits into the calibration $d(\kappa-1) = 1$ (global) and the convex reduction (root system).
- Theorem 3: the convex reduction occurs exactly for $A_2$, $B_2$, $G_2$, $A_1^3$; the node bound gives a short necessity proof for all ranks.
- The observation that Remark 7.4 of the source, read literally, fails for $B_2$ and $G_2$, while Corollary 4 confirms it for the full coincidence.
- Proposition 5 (integrality qualification) with the one-line weight argument and the $\mathrm{SL}_2\times\mathbb{G}_m$ example.
- Theorem 6 and Proposition 7: five of 35 forms; $\mathrm{SO}_4\times\mathrm{GL}_2$ as a self-dual form passing every support test.
- The $\mathrm{GL}_2\times\mathrm{PGL}_2\times\mathrm{PGL}_2$ example with exact counts and Figure 2.

## Strongest rival

The source already names the matching of integrality inequalities with weights as the mechanism and expects it to be exceptional; a specialist may regard the classification as an exercise. Response in the paper: it states exactly what is new (the classification with proof, the counterexamples to the literal remark, the integrality qualification and its consequences for global forms) and what is not (no new period identity, the remaining steps of Theorem 7.1 untouched). A second rival: the integrality condition may be implicit in the source's convention. The journal version's §6.5 remark on descent for (53) suggests the authors track integrality in examples, but Proposition 5.1 as stated permits rational $\check\rho'$; the paper calls Proposition 5 an explicit condition, notes that the source's proof already produces the monomial, and does not call it an error in the published theorems.
